I asked my team one question, and nobody said a word for two minutes: "What are you obsessed with?"

So I went first. Then the room opened up. Adventure. Coding. Things people could talk about for hours.

I borrowed the question from the founder of Wispr Flow, who asks it when he hires. Being good at something makes you capable. Being obsessed with it makes you keep getting better.

If I asked your team, would they have an answer? Would you?

Tell me yours in the comments.
