## Running elements (whole video)
- Background: CU paper / midnight / ultraviolet grounds, moving soft shapes (have them).
- Captions: word-timed, kept **above 1330 px** (HyperFrames safe area), DM Sans.
- Transitions: marigold wipe (have it).
- Music: soft bed, ducked under voice (code-made, or a free track from Pixabay Music if you want something less synthetic, to decide at Stage 3).
