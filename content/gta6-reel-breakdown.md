## Running elements
- **Background:** the pink grid on every scene (slow drift), not the CU dark/purple grounds. Scene 7 dims it for drama.
- **Speaker cue:** whoever is talking gets a marigold ring around their head and a small name tag (Amit / Kapil / Sakshi / Mansi); the others sit slightly smaller.
- **Captions:** Hinglish, word-timed, highlighted word in marigold, kept above 1330 px. Skipped where the same words are big on screen.
- **Transitions:** quick pink-to-white wipes; a hard cut into black for the punchline.
- **Music:** a light, playful bed (code-made), dropping out under scene 7 and the stare.
