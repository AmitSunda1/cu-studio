I asked my team a question that left everyone silent for two minutes.
"What are you obsessed with?"

At first, nobody had an answer.
Some said they weren't obsessed with anything.
Some said, "Work."

Now, I'm not saying they were trying to impress the boss.  
But I wasn't looking for the answer that sounded good in a meeting.

I wanted to know what genuinely excites them.

So I shared what I'm obsessed with and how that obsession connects to growing this company and then the answers started coming.

One person was obsessed with adventure and the experiences that come with it.
Another was obsessed with coding. And honestly, as a company, that's probably one of the best things we could hear. 

Others had their own interests, passions, and things they could talk about endlessly.

Sometimes, we don't know what we're obsessed with until someone asks us the right question.
I came across this idea through a post by the founder Wispr Flow , who includes this question in his hiring process.

It got me thinking about how we look at people when we hire them.

We spend so much time evaluating skills, experience, qualifications, and what someone can bring to the table.
All of that matters.

What if we also tried to understand what drives a person beyond their job?
You don't necessarily have to be obsessed with the company you work for.

You could be obsessed 
With solving problems. 
With building things. 
With adventure. 
With understanding people. 
With getting better at something every single day.

Sometimes, that obsession naturally finds its way into your work.

Being good at something makes you capable of doing it. 
Being obsessed with it makes you want to keep getting better at it.

That difference can take you places that talent alone might not.

So here's a question for you:
If I asked your team what they're obsessed with, would they have an answer?
More importantly, would you know yours?
