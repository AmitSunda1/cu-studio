# **Reel Script: Dear Saurabh Sir & Manish Sir, We're Sorry\!**

**\[Scene 1 – All four sitting together, serious faces, looking directly at the camera\]**

**All together:**

Dear Saurabh Sir and Manish Sir,

Hum aapse dil se maafi maangna chahte hain.

Humein pata hai ki aaj humse ek bahut badi galti ho gayi hai.

Lekin sir, yakeen maaniye, yeh hum nahi the.

Kisi ne humare dimaag par kabza kar liya tha. Hum par vashikaran kiya gaya tha\!

**\[Scene 2 – One by one, explaining what happened\]**

**You:**

Toh hua kuch aisa ki aaj Saurabh Sir aur Manish Sir kisi event ke liye Jaipur gaye hue the.

**Kapil:**

Aur office mein hum chaar masoom log akele reh gaye the.

**Sakshi:**

Tabhi humari nazar padi Sir ke 27-inch wale extra monitor par.

**Mansi:**

Aur bas, phir kya tha... humne monitor ko PlayStation 5 se connect kar diya.

**\[Scene 3 – Dramatic music, show PS5 and monitor setup\]**

**You:**

Lekin Sir, asli kahani toh ab shuru hoti hai.

**Kapil:**

Pata nahi kis shakti ne humein control kiya...

**Sakshi:**

Humne company ke credit card se GTA 6 purchase kar liya\!

**\[Show GTA gameplay, everyone playing, laughing, controller shots\]**

**Mansi:**

Aur phir hum time ka hisaab hi bhool gaye. Kab subah se shaam ho gayi, pata hi nahi chala.

**\[Scene 4 – All four back in front of camera, serious expressions\]**

**You:**

Sir, hum bas itna kehna chahte hain ki yeh sab humari marzi se nahi hua.

**Kapil:**

Humare intentions bilkul acche the.

**Sakshi:**

Hum toh apna EOD update dene hi wale the...

**Mansi:**

Bas EOD mein thoda sa GTA add ho gaya.

**\[Everyone looks at each other. Pause.\]**

**You:**

Aur haan Sir, ek baat aur...

**\[Someone interrupts\]**

GTA 6 bahut achha game hai, Sir\!

**\[Everyone stares at him. Cut to black.\]**
