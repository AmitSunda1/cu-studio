# PhotoSlide

A real photo or product screenshot with a short title. Use it for team stories, behind the scenes and UI reveals.

**JSON:** `{ "type": "photo", "label", "text", "src" }`, with `src` a path to the image.

- Real photos of the CU team and office only. No stock photos of people.
- Screenshots: blur client names unless the client is public.
- Title max 12 words.
